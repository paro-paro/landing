It is a direct connection between two parties. In a banking institution, peer to peer (P2P) does not exist, since there is an intermediary between people, which is the institution itself. In P2P, one person carries out the transaction directly to the other without intermediaries. This is what happens at EthicHub: the investor sends the invested amount directly to the wallet of the Community that requests it.
