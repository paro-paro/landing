[**MetaMask**](https://metamask.io/) is a plugin that acts as a bridge between various dapps* and your web browser without compromising your security, using multiple accounts and without the need to run a full Ethereum node.

*****A DApp (written with this strange D and pronounced as [Di-app]) is a decentralized application, meaning an app that does not depend on a central system but rather depends on the community of users who use it.
