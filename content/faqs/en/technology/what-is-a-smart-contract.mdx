A smart contract, or "SmartContract," is a computer program that facilitates, secures, enforces, and executes agreements recorded between two or more parties (for example, individuals or organizations). As such, they assist in the negotiation and definition of agreements that will cause certain actions to occur as a result of a series of specific conditions being met.
