It is the decentralized technology that enables financial transactions between two participants in a secure, reliable, and irreversible way, without the need for intermediaries. With this technology, absolutely all transactions that occur are recorded. It is public, meaning you can access any of those transactions, while 100% anonymity is maintained. The recorded data is impossible to modify and cannot be deleted.
