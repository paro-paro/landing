Due to the programming of the Smart Contracts, this option is not possible. The maximum you can invest is what each project indicates, and you can invest in as many active projects as you wish. If you want to invest a larger amount, you should contact us at the following email address, where we assist companies or institutions that need to make investments and even purchase the product at the end of the project. [support@ethichub.com](mailto:support@ethichub.com)
