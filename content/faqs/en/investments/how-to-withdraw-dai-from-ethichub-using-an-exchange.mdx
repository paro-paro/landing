1.- Go to Kraken and click on the "Funding" tab.

2.- Click on "Show all active funds".

3.- The different cryptocurrencies that Kraken operates with will appear at the bottom. Click on the star next to DAI to add it to "favorites", so you always have it handy.

4.- Once you have selected it as a "favorite", click on that button, and only DAI will appear. You can choose as many favorite cryptocurrencies as you wish.

The next step is to click on "deposit".

5.- A warning will appear, which you will need to accept.

6.- The following screen will appear, where you should click on "Generate new address".

7.- Next, it will ask for your Kraken operating password.

8.- The address where you need to send your DAI from the EthicHub platform will then appear.

9.- Go to your EthicHub profile, in the "wallet" section, and choose the option: "withdraw DAI".

10.- Enter the amount of DAI you want to transfer, or click MAX if you need to withdraw everything, and paste the address provided by the Exchange in: "Recipient wallet".

Once you have filled in the fields, click "next".

11.- It will ask for the password of your wallet configured on EthicHub, and shortly after you will have the DAI on the Exchange.
