There are different ways, which we detail below:

- If you already have ETH in your wallet, you can exchange them for DAI directly on the EthicHub platform. To do so, go to your profile and in the "wallet" section you will see an option called "I want to exchange my Ether for DAI". It is a very simple and fast process, and in a short time you will have DAI in your wallet ready to be invested in the projects you choose.
- Lastly, if you have neither ETH nor DAI, you can make investments with your credit/debit card, and when the projects are repaid, the capital plus interest will be sent directly to your wallet in DAI.

**IMPORTANT: Please never send DAI directly to the smart contracts; use the platform for this purpose**. Otherwise, the investment will not be registered and cannot be recovered.
