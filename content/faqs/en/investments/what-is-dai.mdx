**IMPORTANT: Please never send DAI directly to the smart contracts; use the platform for this purpose**. Otherwise, the investment will not be registered and cannot be recovered.

DAI is a token deployed on Ethereum, the same blockchain where the EthicHub system is deployed, and [created by Maker](https://makerdao.com/es/). It is designed to follow the value of the USD, which means **using it as the platform currency will significantly reduce investment volatility.**

**To use DAI on the platform, you must grant us permission to manage the DAI in your account.** You can revoke this permission later in "Settings", but you will not be able to invest in that case.

**If you have already invested in a previous project**, the repayment will still be in Ether.
**To continue contributing, you will need to exchange it for DAI.** Fortunately, we will provide an integration with [Paraswap](https://paraswap.io/#/), which will offer the best ETH-DAI exchange route in a transparent and decentralized manner, with the best price available on the market.

**If you want to make an investment,** you can send DAI **through our platform without paying gas**, meaning it will not be necessary to have Ether.

**Our credit card gateway** will work the same as now, but depositing DAI in the smart contract on your behalf, at the exchange rate in effect when EthicHub receives the payment in Euros.
