The communities that request loans through our platform commit to repaying them with interest rates of 16%. 8% goes to the platform user who invested in the project. 4% goes to the local node, responsible for listing the loans on the platform, monitoring them, and ensuring the proper use of the loan funds. The remaining 4% is the platform usage fee and is allocated to purchasing the ethix impact token to strengthen the system designed to handle potential defaults.
