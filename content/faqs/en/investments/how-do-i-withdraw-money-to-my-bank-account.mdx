Once you collect from the project(s) you have invested in, you have the option to withdraw the money from your wallet to your bank.

This option is available in your profile -> wallet -> "withdraw to bank" button.

Make sure you enter the required details correctly, including the amount you want to withdraw and your bank account number.

Once the process is initiated, you will receive the amount you specified within 5 business days, counting from the day after you performed the operation. Transfers will be available only for the SEPA zone.

Remember that if you have any issues, you can contact our support at: [support@ethichub.com](mailto:support@ethichub.com)
