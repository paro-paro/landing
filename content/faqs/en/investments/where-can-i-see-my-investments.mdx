To view your investments, you need to access the platform and in your user area you will find a list of all your transactions by clicking the "transaction history" button.

Here you will see all the transactions made and you can view: the date of the transaction, the payment method, the project status, and when it is time to recover your investment, you can click and go to the project to collect your returns.
