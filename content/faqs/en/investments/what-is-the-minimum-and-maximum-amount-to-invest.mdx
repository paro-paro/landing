The minimum amount is 0.1 xDAI if you invest with your wallet, or if you invest with a card the minimum is 20 (Euro, Dollar, Mexican Peso...).

The maximum amount is the project's funding goal. A single investor can fund an entire project. If you want or are willing to invest an amount greater than a single loan, just write to [support@ethichub.com](mailto:support@ethichub.com).
