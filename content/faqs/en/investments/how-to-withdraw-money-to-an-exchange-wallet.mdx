- Install a wallet app on your phone for the wallet you have chosen (such as "Imtoken", "MyEtherWallet", "Coinomi"... there are many different options).
- When you configure it, do not click on "open/create new wallet". You should choose the "import wallet" option. It will ask for the 12 security words from the wallet created on EthicHub and will sync the data.
- Open an account on an exchange of your choice such as "Kraken", "eToro", "Coinbase",...

Once you have signed up on the exchange, look for the option to "receive money" or "make a deposit" or a similar term (each site will display it with a similar name) and it will show the wallet address where you need to send the money.

- From the app, choose the "send" option, enter the amount of ETH or DAI to transfer, and input the address provided by the exchange.

Once the operation is completed (between a few seconds and a few minutes) you will see it reflected in that wallet.

- If you have xDAI, you have the option to exchange it directly on our platform for DAI, making it easier to send them to the exchange where you have signed up, since not all exchanges accept xDAI.
