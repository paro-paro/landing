EthicHub projects use DAI (a stablecoin pegged to the USD), and previously used Ether, but loans are denominated in the farmers' local currency (Mexican pesos).

Yellow fields indicate an estimate, because Coinmarketcap exchange rates are used at the closest time to the requested date, but rates from other databases may differ.

For a project in progress, an estimate is made of the amount to be withdrawn at the estimated repayment date, using current exchange rates.
