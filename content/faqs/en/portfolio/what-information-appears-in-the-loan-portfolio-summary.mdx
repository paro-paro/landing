In your portfolio summary, you can see all the data from your investments in EthicHub projects.

**Returned:** Summary of all successful repayments at the time they were made and at the exchange rate of that moment.

**Pending:** Summary of data from funded projects generating interest pending maturity, at today's exchange rate.

**Invested:** Summary of your investments with the exchange rate at the time of investment.

**Profits:** Summary of withdrawals corresponding to the exchange rate at the time of project repayment by the farmers.

For projects pending repayment, the profits are those expected at their repayment date assuming no defaults and at today's exchange rate.

NOTE: The amount in FIAT currencies (Euro, Dollar...) may differ at the time of withdrawal from the project to the wallet, as exchange rates continue to fluctuate after loan repayment.

**Annual Return:** Annualized average return across all projects. It is a weighted average, giving more weight to projects with higher investment volume.
