Yes. By clicking on each project, you will see all the information about what has happened since you invested.

## **Dates**:

These are the date the project was funded and the repayment date (estimated if the project is In Progress, or actual if it has been Returned).

## **Financing:**

Information regarding the funding process.

**Financing:** Information regarding the funding process.

**Invested:** The amount invested displayed in the selected currency, at the exchange rate at the time of investment. The investment date considered is when the transaction is confirmed in the Smart Contract.

**Sent to communities:** Principal in the loan Smart Contract currency (DAI for new projects, ETH for older projects). There may be variations from the principal due to exchange rate fluctuations during the funding process, and due to promotions (whose amount is added here).

**Outbound exchange rate:** The exchange rate from the Smart Contract currency to the farmers' local currency.

**Principal MX$:** The amount in local currency that you lend to the farmers, which is the basis for interest calculation.

## **Interest:**

Here you can see the project's annual interest rate. The effective interest is what you receive when the farmer repays the loan, and the estimated interest is calculated at the estimated repayment date. We differentiate them since some projects may not be annual in duration, and the calculation is based on the project's actual duration.

## **Return:**

Here you will find the amount the farmer repays, with currency conversions.

**In local currency**: Principal + interest generated in the farmers' currency.

**In the project currency:** Local currency principal divided by the return exchange rate.

**In the selected currency:** Conversion at the exchange rate of the selected currency. If the project has been Returned, the exchange rate at the time of the project's repayment is used. If it is still In Progress, the current exchange rate is used.

**Return exchange rate:** Used by the farmers to repay the project.

## **Profitability:**

Data and estimates about the project's profitability.

**Profits:** Return - Investment, in the selected currency.

**Effective return:** Profits / Investment %.

**Forex influence:** Difference between the effective return and the interest generated in the local currency (MXN). A measure of the difference between profitability and interest, caused by exchange rate fluctuations in the funding process, DAI (or Ether in older projects) against the currency requested by the investor, exchange spreads, and the evolution of the local currency (MXN) between funding and repayment.

**Annualized return**: Effective return, as if the project had lasted 365 days.

Annualized forex influence: Forex influence, as if the project had lasted 365 days.
