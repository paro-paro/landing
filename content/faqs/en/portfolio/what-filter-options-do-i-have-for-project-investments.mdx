**Accepting contributions:** The investments in the project have not yet reached 100%, so the funds have not been sent to the farmers.

**In progress:** Project fully funded and delivered to the farmers. Accruing interest.

**Returned:** The farmer has already repaid the loan and it is time to recover your investment plus returns.

**Not funded:** The project did not receive the necessary investments to reach 100% and the funds raised cannot be sent to the farming community. You can withdraw your funds if you have not done so yet.

**All:** Here you can see all the projects in your portfolio without differentiating by the categories above.
