In the portfolio, you can see the evolution of your loan investments throughout the project's lifecycle, as well as track the influence of exchange rates on expected and obtained returns.

The transaction history contains all individual deposit and withdrawal transactions from your wallet to the credit projects and vice versa, as well as links to receipts on the Ethereum blockchain (displayed through the block explorer [Etherscan](https://etherscan.com))
