It appears in the upper left corner (Wallet Value) and is the amount you have available to make your next investment in any of the projects currently accepting contributions. It accounts for the amount of DAI (and Ether, if applicable) you have available.

You can click on the amount to access the "Wallet Information" section, with a breakdown of currencies and related actions such as withdrawals, deposits, exchanges, and if you use an EthicHub wallet, you can access your private key and 12 backup words with your wallet key.

NOTE: Please keep your private key and 12 backup words in a safe place. They are the only way to recover access to your wallet and **are under your personal custody**. **EthicHub does not have a copy.**
