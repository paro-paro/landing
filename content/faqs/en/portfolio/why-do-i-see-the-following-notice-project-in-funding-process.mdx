Projects in the funding process do not yet generate interest. The principal of your investment will be set once the project is fully funded and converted to the farmers' local currency, at which point the investment principal is determined and interest begins to accrue.

For this reason, there may be fluctuations in your investment from the time you invest until the project is fully funded.

If the project is not fully funded, it will move to the "Not Funded" status and you will be notified so you can withdraw your funds from the project to your wallet. This is not an automatic operation as it requires a security signature with your wallet key. The withdrawal transaction costs are covered by EthicHub.
