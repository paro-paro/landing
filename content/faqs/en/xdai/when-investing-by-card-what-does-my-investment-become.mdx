Upon receiving the card payment, our system will apply the corresponding exchange rate to DAI and issue a transaction on the xDAI blockchain that deposits the corresponding value into the desired project, in the name of the user's wallet.

It works the same as before with Dai or Ether, but now with xDai on a different blockchain.
