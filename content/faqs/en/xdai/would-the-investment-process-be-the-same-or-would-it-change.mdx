For users with an EthicHub-generated wallet, it will be very similar and you will be able to continue making investments. And if your method is to invest by card, remember that you will continue with the same process and your investment will be linked to your wallet.

MetaMask users will need to configure it according to these instructions:

** **[https://www.xdaichain.com/for-users/wallets/metamask/metamask-setup](https://www.xdaichain.com/for-users/wallets/metamask/metamask-setup)

The costs of investing in a project will be those of performing transactions on xDAI (about 4 decimal places of USD, 0.0001 USD), which will be subtracted from the amount sent in the investment.

We apologize for the inconvenience (we all like round numbers) but due to technical limitations, it is the user's own wallet that must make these movements.
