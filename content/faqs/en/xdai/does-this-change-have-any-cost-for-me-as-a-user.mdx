The costs of moving DAI from Ethereum to xDAI are covered by EthicHub.

The costs of moving xDAI to Ethereum and investing in a project will be those of performing transactions on xDAI (in the order of 4 decimal places of USD, 0.0001 USD), which will be subtracted from the tokens sent.

We apologize for the inconvenience (we all like round numbers) but due to technical limitations, it is the user's own wallet that must make these movements.
