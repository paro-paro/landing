The Ethereum network is currently congested, as the demand to include transactions exceeds the network's capacity. Because of this, the difficulty of the work performed by validator nodes (miners) is greater, resulting in very high transaction and smart contract deployment costs. Attempting to submit a transaction at a lower price than the current demand may result in hours of waiting or even the transaction not being executed at all.

This situation will persist until Ethereum develops its version 2.0 to solve its scalability problem.

For the sake of usability and financial inclusion, at EthicHub we cover our users' transaction costs whenever possible (except for transactions to external wallets).

To operate sustainably and provide better service to our users, we decided to move our operations from Ethereum to xDAI for the following reasons:

- Cheaper and faster transactions

The consensus mechanism of xDAI is Proof of Authority, which is less costly for validator nodes, resulting in very low network fees that are also paid in xDAI (just cents of USD). Since there is no bidding to include transactions, confirmation times are shorter.

- Same currency value

- Despite being on 2 different blockchains, 1 xDAI = 1 DAI, so the migration will not be noticeable in the user's wallet value.

- Liquidity and connection with Ethereum

- Thanks to the "token bridges" between Ethereum and xDAI, xDAI can be redeemed for DAI at any time.

- Same smart contracts

- The experience is similar for users and developers.
