Projects prior to and including SEE NUMBER were repaid in DAI on the Ethereum network. If you have pending investment recovery, you will be able to do so in DAI on Ethereum.

From that project onwards, your investment will be returned in xDAI on the network of the same name.

Remember that 1 DAI = 1 xDAI in economic value.
