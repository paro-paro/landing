To convert xDAI from the xDAI blockchain to Ethereum, turning them into DAI, the xDAI team provides a website that facilitates the transition.

The process consists of 2 transactions: one on the xDAI side where the xDAI is sent to the bridge, and another on the Ethereum side to withdraw the DAI to your wallet. This transaction is necessary and has an execution cost of **at least** **0.0109 ETH, depending on the degree of network congestion.**

To use the xDai chain bridge, you need a MetaMask wallet or one compatible with WalletConnect and xDAI. [Here are the instructions for using the bridge, including a video](https://www.xdaichain.com/for-users/bridges/converting-xdai-via-bridge/moving-xdai-to-dai)

If you don't have MetaMask, follow the [instructions](https://metamask.io/download.html) to install it and send your xDAI to that address from the corresponding functionality page.
