From EthicHub, you can migrate your entire DAI balance by selecting the corresponding option in your Profile > Wallet > Migrate DAI / xDAI section. To convert DAI to xDAI with your EthicHub wallet, you will need a minimum of 25 DAI.

If you have MetaMask, you can use this website from POA Network itself (note: the transaction costs through this method are borne by the user, so you will need ETH in your wallet): [https://dai-bridge.poa.network/](https://dai-bridge.poa.network/)
