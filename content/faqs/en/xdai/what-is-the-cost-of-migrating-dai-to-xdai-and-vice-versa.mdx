The costs of moving DAI from Ethereum to xDAI are covered by EthicHub.

The costs of investing in a project will be those of performing transactions on xDAI (in the order of 4 decimal places of USD, 0.0001 USD), which will be subtracted from the tokens sent.

We apologize for the inconvenience (we all like round numbers) but due to technical limitations, it is the user's own wallet that must make these movements. The cost of withdrawing xDAI to DAI consists of one transaction on xDAI and one transaction on Ethereum to recover the tokens, with a cost dependent on the state of the Ethereum network (usually more than 0.01 ETH). As this is an external service, EthicHub has no control over it, but we offer an xDAI to EUR withdrawal or the option to send xDAI to wallets on the same network.
