Yes. Your wallet is the same and you will be able to operate with it as you have done so far.

You will simply need to add the xDAI network to your MetaMask networks.

[https://www.xdaichain.com/for-users/wallets/metamask/metamask-setup](https://www.xdaichain.com/for-users/wallets/metamask/metamask-setup)

MetaMask guide:

1) Open MetaMask and select "Custom RPC" from the network dropdown menu.

2) In the "Custom RPC" settings, add the xDai network details and click Save:

Network Name: xDai

New RPC URL: https://rpc.xdaichain.com/

Chain ID: 0x64 (100)

Symbol: xDai

Block Explorer URL: https://blockscout.com/poa/xdai

Note: MetaMask recently updated the ChainID to be a required field. When you update, you may need to re-enter the chain identifier 100, and it will be converted to hexadecimal: 0x64.

If you have issues, try entering 100 for Chain ID and save the settings again.
