EthicHub is a crowdlending platform for investors and farmers. At the same time, we have a marketplace for purchases made by large coffee companies.

We connect farmers who lack access to bank loans with investors seeking higher returns or wanting to generate social impact. We also close coffee purchase contracts with large companies. This way, loan repayment is guaranteed for the investor from the start, farmers can finance themselves at a lower cost while increasing their production, and large companies have their annual coffee purchases secured.
