The difference is that we don't just seek investments; one of our goals is to generate social impact in the communities that request loans. This social impact is created because the loans carry lower interest rates for the farmer, which means they can obtain a higher margin when selling their coffee. Another important point is that through our marketplace, we close contracts with large companies, thereby guaranteeing loan repayment by the farmers.
