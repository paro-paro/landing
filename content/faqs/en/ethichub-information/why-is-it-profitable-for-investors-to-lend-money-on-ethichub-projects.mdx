It is not easy to find financial products in developed countries that offer good returns with moderate risk.

EthicHub offers a crowdlending platform where loans are directed toward financing real projects, based on the real economy, for small farming communities made up of hardworking people with highly productive activities.

Through our platform, we channel this financing by offering much cheaper and more accessible interest rates to farmers, enabling them to comfortably repay their loans.

Our investments have an annual return of 8% in xDai/US dollar, with the added satisfaction of helping these people break the cycle of poverty and develop socially and economically.
