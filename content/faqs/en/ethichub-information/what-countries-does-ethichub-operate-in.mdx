We are currently financing projects for communities in Mexico, Brazil, Honduras, and Peru, with plans to expand to other countries. These will always be communities that need investment to continue meeting their agricultural production needs. For investors, we operate in all countries where this type of investment is accepted.

For investors: We operate in all countries where this type of investment is accepted (except in the USA and Mexico).
