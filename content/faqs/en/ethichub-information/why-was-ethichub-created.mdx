EthicHub was created to provide two solutions. The first is to minimize the high interest rates that unbanked farmers pay when repaying loans. The second is to offer higher returns to investors, since standard financial plans currently would not give investors more than a 2% return. This means that both farmers and investors benefit, while also generating social impact in coffee-producing communities.
