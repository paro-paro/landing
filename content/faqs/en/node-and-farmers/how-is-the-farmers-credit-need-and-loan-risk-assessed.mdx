The Local Node that helps farmers group together to apply for a collective loan knows the production process and visits the land to assess whether the requested amounts correlate with the expected harvests. In the first year, the loan is only for the harvest, so the amount is modest. Once the farmers complete the first production cycle, they can apply for a significantly larger amount and much earlier in order to invest in improving the productivity of their land.
