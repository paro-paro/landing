The Local Node is the person who visits communities to offer EthicHub's services, explains the operating conditions, helps them organize into a collective for the loan application, validates the information provided by the farmers, and supervises the proper use of the funds.
One of EthicHub's main tasks is the search, selection, training, supervision, and support of new local nodes. There is a local node manual to assist them in their work.
