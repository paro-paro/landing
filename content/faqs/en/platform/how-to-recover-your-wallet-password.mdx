1. Go to your profile -> wallet and you will see the button "forgot your wallet password?" at the bottom. Click on it.

2. It will then ask you to enter the 12 security words (seed). They must be entered in the same order in which they appeared when you created the wallet, separated by a space, and respecting uppercase, lowercase letters, and accents. Once written, you must enter a new password and repeat it in the next field. As you type the password, a colored bar will fill up, indicating its security level: low - medium - high.

3. Once everything is filled in, click "import wallet".

4. If the password is not secure enough or does not match when repeated, the corresponding messages will appear: "a more secure password is needed" or "passwords do not match".

5. If everything is correct, the initial screen of the "wallet" section will be displayed and a message will appear at the top: "wallet imported".
