Within each project there is a box with its characteristics and needs. In that box there is a button that indicates:

- Contribute: to transfer your investment to the Community and participate in the funding. Depending on whether you pay with Dai or directly with a card, the investment process may vary slightly. In any case, when finished and if everything goes correctly, your investment will appear with the status "success" in your user area.
- Recover: when the funding end date arrives, using this button you can recover both the invested capital and the generated interest. It will be returned to your wallet at the moment you request the recovery amount.
