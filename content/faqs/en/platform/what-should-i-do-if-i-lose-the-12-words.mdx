If you have your **wallet created directly** on our website, it is extremely important that you keep the 12 words you obtained in a safe place.

If you registered on our website **after May 26, 2021**, you can access it with your email password.

If you use **Metamask** as your wallet, remember to also save the 12 words you were given when creating the wallet.
