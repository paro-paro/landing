title: "What should I do if I lose my access password?"
description: ""
icon: "circle-help"
section: "platform"
sectionTitle: "Platform"
order: 10
---

If you have lost your platform access password, you can regenerate it by going to the "Log in" screen and clicking "Forgot your password?". An email will be sent to you and you will be able to reset it.

For the wallet, the only solution is to go through the process of entering the 12 key words that the system will ask for in order to access the wallet. That is why it is very important to keep the 12 words in a safe and easy-to-remember place.
