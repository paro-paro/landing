This could be a **very important point** that you should pay **close attention to**. Each wallet is personal and owned by each user. To access it, you will need to enter your username and password. But if you have forgotten your wallet access password, these words will be your allies to unlock access.

Keep the 12 words safe and do not lose them, ideally on a physical medium (paper, cardboard)...
