sectionTitle: "Platform"

---

1. Go to your profile -> wallet and you will see the "Advanced" button at the bottom. Click on it.

2. Two new buttons will appear:

a) Show the 12 backup words

b) Show private key

Click on the option you wish to recover. We always recommend having both the seed (12 words) and the private key written down, since if you lose one you have the other option to access the wallet.

3. In the next step, it will ask for your Wallet password. Once entered, click "unlock".

4. The requested information will then be displayed.

- Either the 12 words

- or the private key

We always emphasize, and it is very important, that they should never be stored on devices susceptible to hacking (PCs, phones, tablets...), and should never be shown to anyone else. It is recommended to write them down on paper, always kept in a locatable place, and to have at least one copy.

Security is fundamental.
