The 12 words are used solely and exclusively to configure/import the wallet. Whether within our platform or in any other mobile wallet application. As we always mention, although they are only used very sporadically, they must always be available, since if they are lost there is no way to recover that wallet.

The wallet password is what allows you to perform all operations with it: contribute, recover, change the wallet password... Drawing a parallel with bank accounts, it is like the PIN to authorize transactions.
