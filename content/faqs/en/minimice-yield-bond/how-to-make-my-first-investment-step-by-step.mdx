The steps you should follow are:

-
Have a Metamask wallet or one compatible with WalletConnect. If you don't, you can create one at [www.metamask.io](http://www.metamask.io).

- Have in that wallet the amount of DAI you wish to invest and enough ETH to pay for gas. If you don't have cryptocurrencies, you can buy ETH directly from Metamask with a bank card, set aside a small amount to pay for gas, and with the rest, perform a swap from ETH to DAI.
- Connect your wallet to the ethix.ethichub.com protocol, making sure your wallet is on the Ethereum network (Ethereum Mainnet).
- Select one of the three bonds and click BUY BOND.
- Enter the amount to invest in DAI, confirm the term, and click NEXT.
- If this is the first bond you are purchasing, you will need to approve (APPROVE) the use of DAI in your wallet by signing the transaction in your Metamask wallet.
- Once the transaction is approved, click MINT MiniMice BOND and accept the Terms and Conditions.
- Confirm the transaction and pay the gas cost in the Metamask wallet.
- Wait for the bond to be executed and minted (it may take a few minutes).
- Once completed, you can go to My Bonds to view all your positions.
