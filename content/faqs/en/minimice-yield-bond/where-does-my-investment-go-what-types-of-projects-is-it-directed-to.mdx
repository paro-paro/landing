The liquidity obtained through MiniMice is allocated to financing projects for smallholder farmers in developing countries who lack access to formal financing. The projects will be visible on the lending platform at [www.ethichub.com](http://www.ethichub.com). Liquidity not invested in harvest cycles will be placed in fixed-term funds in stablecoins across different blockchain ecosystems.
