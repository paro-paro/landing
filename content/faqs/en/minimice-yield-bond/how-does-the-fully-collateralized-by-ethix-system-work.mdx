When your bond is generated, you receive a token or NFT that acts as a payment right for the principal plus interest at the maturity date: once the term has ended, the user can claim the invested amount plus the returns, receiving the owed amount in DAI.

If the bond were declared as defaulted at its maturity date, the system automatically executes the guarantee and delivers 5 Ethix for each DAI invested.
