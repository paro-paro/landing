The minimum amount to invest is 1 DAI (1 USD), but you should consider the additional transaction cost on the Ethereum network (gas), which depending on network congestion can exceed 10 dollars. Therefore, the recommended minimum investment is 100 DAI (USD). We recommend always checking the Ethereum gas cost on a block explorer beforehand.
