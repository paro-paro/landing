Ethix aims to serve as a bridge between DeFi and the real economy represented by the more than one billion small farmers worldwide. Even without collateral, after more than two years of operation, loans on the EthicHub platform maintain a 0% default rate. By capitalizing a Compensation Reserve with the majority of Ethix, we will provide collateral for these farmers' loans, further facilitating their financing by reducing perceived risk, while simultaneously creating an incentive loop: the more investors participate, the more value Ethix will capture, the more value will be in the Compensation Reserve generating greater security, and therefore it will attract more investors again, completing the loop.

More info: [https://docs-ethix.ethichub.com/ethix/token-ethix](https://docs-ethix.ethichub.com/ethix/token-ethix)
