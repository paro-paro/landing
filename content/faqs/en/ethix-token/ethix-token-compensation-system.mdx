In the extraordinary event that loans do not produce the minimum expected return, the loan's **Smart Contract** will automatically liquidate the necessary Ethix from the **Compensation System**, allocating them to investors affected by such abnormality.

The Compensation System comprises 4 levels:

- **Loan Originator Hub:** To originate loans on EthicHub, it is required to **lock** Ethix in the defined volumes, in addition to passing the *due diligence* of a specialized committee that validates the capacity to handle the new loan demand, the risk of said loans, and the positive impact for small farmers. The Ethix from the Originator Hubs remain as collateral for their projects, and in case of default, they are the first to be liquidated to compensate affected lenders.

- **The Compensation Reserve:** When the Ethix locked by the Originator Hub are not sufficient, a Smart Contract will request the global Compensation Reserve to sell Ethix in exchange for DAI until the expected return is covered.

- **Ethix locked by their holders:** Ethix holders can also contribute to mitigating ecosystem risks by locking Ethix that will serve as a third level of collateral in the unlikely event that the Ethix from the Originator Hub combined with the Ethix from the Compensation Reserve are not sufficient to cover the default. At this third level, liquidity providers within decentralized exchange pools (with EthicHub/stablecoin pairs) can also be counted, as they may opt to be part of the Compensation System at an additional level, if they decide to lock their positions within the pool (in Ethix and DAI) and receive in return the same benefits as those participating in the third level of compensation (in addition to the benefits they receive as liquidity providers per se).

- **Ethix/DAI pool liquidity providers:** Liquidity providers within decentralized exchange pools (with EthicHub/stablecoin pairs) may opt to be part of the Compensation System at a fourth level, if they decide to lock their positions within the pool (in Ethix and DAI) and receive in return the same benefits as those participating in the third level of compensation (in addition to the benefits they receive as liquidity providers per se).
