On Bilaxy or Uniswap the established pair is ETHIX/Ether, so yes. If you use either of these two platforms, you will need to have Ether. On Balancer the pair is ETHIX/DAI, so in that case you would need to have DAI in your wallet. However, in all cases except Bilaxy, you also need to have Ether to pay for the gas generated by transactions and smart contract execution.
