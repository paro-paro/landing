It is a term that emerged with blockchain technology and the possibilities of decentralized finance (DeFi). We could translate it as "locking." Users who stake their tokens, through a smart contract, can benefit from providing liquidity on various protocols and receiving fees, among other advantages. However, these are not recommended operations for users who are not fully familiar with the ecosystem.

Currently, you can stake your Ethix tokens easily on the EthicHub website created for this purpose: [https://ethix.ethichub.com/es/stake](https://ethix.ethichub.com/es/stake), or on Balancer or Uniswap.
