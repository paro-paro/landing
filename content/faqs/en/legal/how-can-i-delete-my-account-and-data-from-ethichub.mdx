If you wish to cancel your EthicHub account, send an email to [support@ethichub.com](mailto:support@ethichub.com) or [rgdp@ethichub.com](mailto:rgdp@ethichub.com) with the subject "Delete account" and we will begin the process of deleting your account and data within our technical and legal capabilities.
