Under Spanish legislation. We are not a participatory financing platform from the perspective of the law, as we source 100% of the projects outside of Spain. We use cryptocurrencies and smart contracts for value transfers. Although we use cryptocurrencies, you can also invest with a card and your country's currency.

For investors: We operate in all countries where this type of investment is accepted (except in the USA and Mexico).
