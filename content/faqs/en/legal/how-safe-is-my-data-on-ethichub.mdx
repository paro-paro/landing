The security and privacy of our customers' data is of utmost importance to EthicHub.

- As part of our business, EthicHub must collect some personal identification data. Both EthicHub and our partners operate with the highest levels of security and compliance.
- EthicHub loans work through smart contracts on the Ethereum blockchain, one of the most secure in the world. The contracts have been audited by independent third parties.
- Within the wallet you will find bank details, credit card data, private keys, and the 12 words of your wallet.

You can also consult our [privacy policy](https://ethichub.com/about/privacy-policy).
