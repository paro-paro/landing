description: ""

sectionTitle: "Investment Risks"

---

For a project to be declared in default, a **maximum of 90 days must elapse from its maturity date**. Defaulted projects are covered by our compensation system, so the person who participated will still receive their initial capital plus the interest generated up to the date the default is declared.
