**Affordable loans to improve production and logistics are easily repaid thanks to access to international markets for the financed crops** (EthicHub earns a 4% commission on each loan and a margin on marketing the green coffee).

Cutting-edge technology allows **both crowdlenders and institutional lenders** to **participate in a shared value model by connecting complementary economic regions** (global North and South). It also has a high entry barrier: banks cannot offer marketing services and traders cannot provide financing.
