Under this framework, each participant acts in collective interest because it serves their own:

- **Investors** join to obtain a return while generating social and environmental impact
- **Borrowers** repay because securing favorable loan terms and international market entry becomes accessible
- **Auditors** endorse trustworthy cooperatives as their core responsibility involves demonstrating measurable results

<Callout variant="tip">
**In the regenerative economy, everyone wins.**
</Callout>
