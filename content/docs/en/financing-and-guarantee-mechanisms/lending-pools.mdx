Lending Pools represent contributions from several participants to a common fund that finances the lines of credit for agricultural Originators in the EthicHub system.

These investments support agricultural production, crop processing, and export activities.

## Key Investment Details

Participants have flexibility in their contributions. The minimum amount to invest is $50 USD, with maturity options of 6, 12, or 24 months. Longer investment periods offer better returns. At maturity, investors recover their principal plus earned interest.

## Current and Future Structures

**Current Version (Continuous Interest):**
When maturity expires without withdrawal, the investment continues generating interest under the original terms.

**Upcoming Version (Withdrawal Period):**
A planned enhancement will introduce a redemption window upon loan maturity. Under this system, investors will have 30 days to withdraw funds. If funds remain unredeemed after this period, they automatically roll into a new investment cycle matching the original duration.
